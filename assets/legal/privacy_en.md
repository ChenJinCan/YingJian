# Yingjian Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Yingjian uses on-device image processing by default. This policy explains how the current foundation handles photos, editing data, and optional diagnostics.

## 1. Photos and editing content

Selected photos, local masks, editing recipes, and exported results are processed on the device by default. Browsing recommendations, moving adjustment controls, and using local editing tools do not upload photos.

If cloud AI editing is introduced, the app will separately explain which photo or region is required, why it is needed, and any cost before asking for explicit confirmation. Declining cloud processing will not disable local editing.

## 2. Optional diagnostics

Anonymous diagnostics are off by default and can be enabled or disabled in Settings. When enabled, Google Firebase may process:

- Analytics: allowlisted screens, feature stages, and success, failure, or cancellation outcomes;
- Crashlytics: app version, device and operating environment, minimized error types, and stack traces;
- Performance Monitoring: startup and operation duration, device and app version, and aggregated network URL patterns without query parameters.

Custom analytics events, performance attributes, and crash reasons must not contain photos, file paths, face data, prompts, editing text, account identifiers, precise location, or raw error messages.

## 3. Ratings and stores

After enough successful editing activity, the operating system may show its native review prompt subject to Apple or Google Play quotas. Ratings and reviews are processed by the applicable store. The persistent Rate this app action only opens the store listing.

## 4. Permissions

Photo access is used only to select images for editing and save results you explicitly export. The app should request a permission only immediately before the related capability and explain its purpose.

When you actively use voice editing, microphone access is used only to capture that instruction and speech-recognition access is used only to turn it into editable text. Depending on the device, system version, and language support, transcription may be provided by Apple's system speech-recognition service. Yingjian does not send the photo together with the voice instruction. Denying or disabling these permissions does not affect typed instructions or manual editing.

## 5. Third-party processors

When diagnostics are enabled, Google Firebase provides analytics, crash reporting, and performance monitoring. App stores process ratings, reviews, downloads, and purchase-related data under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 6. Retention, opt-out, and deletion

Disabling diagnostics stops new custom analytics and performance reporting. Firebase SDKs may handle pending crash records created before collection was disabled according to their documented behavior. Diagnostic data is retained according to the Firebase project configuration and service rules.

Yingjian currently has no account system and does not store photos on developer-operated servers. Deleting the app clears its local app data. For questions or requests relating to app data, use the developer support channel shown on the app store listing.

## 7. Children's privacy

Yingjian is not designed specifically for children and does not intentionally collect children's personal content through custom telemetry events.

## 8. Policy updates

We will update this policy when data handling, third-party SDKs, or cloud features materially change and will request renewed consent where required.
